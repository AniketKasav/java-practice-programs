TOPIC4: Aptitude Question
1. Basic Average
The average of 5 numbers is 20. If four of the numbers are 18, 22, 19, and 21, find
the fifth number.
2. Consecutive Numbers
Find the average of first 10 natural numbers.
3. Change in Average
The average age of 6 students is 15 years. If one more student aged 21 joins
them, what is the new average?
4. Weighted Average
A student scored 60, 70, and 80 marks in three subjects. Find the average marks.
5. Average Speed
A car travels 60 km at 30 km/h and another 60 km at 60 km/h. Find the average
speed of the journey.
6. Missing Value
The average of 8 numbers is 25. If one number is removed, the average becomes
24. Find the removed number.
7. Combined Average
The average weight of 5 boys is 50 kg and that of 3 girls is 45 kg. Find the
combined average weight.
8. Age Problem
The average age of a family of 4 members is 30 years. A new member is born.
What is the new average age after 2 years?
9. Increase in Average
The average of 10 numbers is 40. If each number is increased by 5, what will be
the new average?
10. Team Average
The average score of a cricket team of 11 players is 45 runs. If the captain scored
90 runs, find the average score of the remaining players.
